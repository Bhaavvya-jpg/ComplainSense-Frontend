ComplainSense
Smart complaint prioritisation based on urgency and severity.

People submit complaints (electricity, water, internet, security, ...) and rate how urgent and how severe each one is (1-5). ComplainSense calculates a priority score, ranks all complaints, and shows administrators what to handle first.

Educational decision-support tool, not a validated safety assessment. Scores come from self-reported ratings. Complaints in emergency categories (Security, Fire Safety, Medical, Electrical Hazard) are always flagged for immediate human review, whatever their score.
